# AI Use Case Intake

## Purpose

This document defines the AI use-case intake process for the AI Governance Control Tower project.

The intake process captures the minimum information needed before an AI-enabled use case can move into risk assessment, service mapping, control review, and approval.

The purpose is not to block AI adoption. The purpose is to make AI adoption visible, reviewable, governable, and operationally accountable.

---

## Intake Objective

The intake process answers four basic questions:

1. What is the business trying to do?
2. Why is AI being considered?
3. What data, users, systems, and stakeholders are involved?
4. What risks or controls must be reviewed before proceeding?

---

## When Intake Is Required

AI use-case intake is required when a team wants to:

- Build or deploy a generative AI solution
- Use an AWS AI service in a business workflow
- Add AI-assisted decision support
- Automate document review, summarization, classification, or extraction
- Use AI to interact with customers, employees, vendors, or regulated processes
- Connect AI to confidential, regulated, or business-critical data
- Move an AI prototype toward pilot or production
- Review an existing AI use case that was not previously governed

---

## Intake Inputs

The intake process should capture:

- Use case name
- Business unit or function
- Business owner
- Requestor
- Business problem
- Reason AI is being considered
- Expected business value
- Success criteria
- AI capability type
- Expected outputs
- Human oversight model
- Stakeholders
- Affected users or customers
- Data sources
- Data classification
- Known data quality concerns
- AWS AI or cloud services under consideration
- Initial risk areas
- Required reviewers
- Operational owner
- Monitoring expectations
- Escalation triggers
