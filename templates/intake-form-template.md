# AI Use Case Intake Form Template

## Purpose

This template is used to capture the minimum information required before an AI-enabled use case can move into governance review.

The goal is not to slow down innovation. The goal is to make sure the organization understands the business purpose, stakeholders, data, risks, controls, and operational ownership before AI is deployed.
